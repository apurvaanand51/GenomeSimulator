# Genome model

The project uses a virtual genome designed for educational interaction. The genome is composed of 12 genes and each gene has a synthetic DNA sequence, a protein association, affected traits, and a pathway.

The goal is to keep every gene connected to a realistic conceptual relationship, even though the model is fictional. Example relationship:

- Flight speed is tied to the flight gene and functional wing morphology.
- Metabolism changes affect energy use and stress tolerance.
- Lifespan and reproduction interact through the growth and maintenance pathway.

The engine deliberately uses simulated mutations rather than real-world genomic engineering logic.
