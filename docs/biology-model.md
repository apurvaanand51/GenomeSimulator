# Biology model

This model is intentionally educational and uses simplified algebraic relationships.

Example equations:

- flight_speed = base_flight_speed × flight_gene_factor × muscle_efficiency
- energy_consumption = base_energy_consumption × flight_speed_factor × metabolism_factor
- reproduction_rate = baseline × reproductive_signal

These are not claims about real mosquito biology. They are design heuristics for an interactive teaching tool.

## Parameter calculations

- Body mass depends on growth gene effects.
- Wing area responds to wing morphology and size traits.
- Energy is moderated by the virtual energy pathway.
- Lifespan is an output of growth and maintenance signals.
- Temperature tolerance is affected by thermal-response and stress pathway genes.
