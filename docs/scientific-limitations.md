# Scientific limitations

## Known biological information

- Mosquitoes have real genes and biochemical pathways.
- Phenotypes arise from complex developmental and ecological interactions.
- Real biology includes gene regulation, epigenetics, and environmental feedback.

## Simplified assumptions

- The model uses a small finite set of virtual genes.
- The DNA sequences are synthetic and educational.
- Trait-to-gene mapping is deterministic and intentionally simplified.
- Protein effects are abstract and not physically folded.

## Virtual simulation

This application demonstrates a causal lab workflow but does not claim to be a predictive experiment tool for real mosquitoes.

## Future real-data integration

In future versions, scientifically sourced data sources can be layered in through validated annotations, pathway models, and curated biological datasets.
